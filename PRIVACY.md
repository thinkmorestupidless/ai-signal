# Privacy policy

*AI Signal, Chrome extension. Last updated 2026-10-21.*

AI Signal does not collect, store, transmit or share any data about you or about the pages
you read.

**What it reads.** On LinkedIn and Reddit only, the extension reads the text of posts and
comments shown on the page in order to score them. That reading happens inside the page, in
your browser, and the text goes nowhere else.

**What it stores.** Your settings (on or off, minimum word count, whether to show the reasons,
whether to score comments, the hide threshold) are kept in Chrome's extension storage, which
Chrome may sync between your own signed-in browsers. Nothing else is stored.

**What it sends.** Nothing. The extension makes no network requests, loads no remote code and
contains no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.

**Third parties.** None. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising, credit or lending
decisions, or any purpose unrelated to scoring posts in the page.

**Permissions.** `storage`, for the settings above. Host access to `www.linkedin.com`,
`www.reddit.com`, `old.reddit.com` and `new.reddit.com`, so the content script can read posts
on those sites and insert the badge. No other sites are touched.

**Removing it.** Uninstalling the extension removes its settings. There is nothing else to
delete, because nothing else was kept.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/thinkmorestupidless/ai-signal.
